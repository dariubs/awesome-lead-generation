## Prospecting & Data Enrichment

- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/) - Prospecting database with built-in outreach sequencing.
- [Clay](https://www.clay.com/) - Combines dozens of data sources with AI enrichment and waterfalls in a spreadsheet-like interface.
- [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/) - Large B2B contact and intent-data platform, enterprise-focused.
- [Clearbit](https://clearbit.com/) - Company and person enrichment API, now part of HubSpot's Breeze Intelligence.
- [Hunter](https://hunter.io/) - Email finder and verifier.
- [Lusha](https://www.lusha.com/) - Contact and company data with a browser extension for prospecting.
- [Cognism](https://www.cognism.com/) - Compliant B2B contact data with strong EMEA phone coverage.
- [RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/) - Contact lookup by name, company, or LinkedIn profile.
- [ATS Hiring Signals](https://apify.com/goldenrod_serrano/ats-hiring-signals) - Apify Actor that lists open jobs and a hiring score per company from 6 ATS job boards (paid, per result).
- [Workday Hiring Signals](https://apify.com/goldenrod_serrano/workday-hiring-signals) - Apify Actor that lists open jobs on Workday career sites by country, with a hiring summary (paid, per result).

## Email Outreach & Sequencing

- [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/) - Cold email sending at scale with inbox rotation and warmup.
- [Lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com/) - Personalized cold email sequences with image/video personalization.
- [Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/) - Multi-inbox cold email infrastructure built for agencies and scale.
- [Outreach](https://www.outreach.io/) - Enterprise sales engagement platform.
- [Salesloft](https://salesloft.com/) - Sales engagement and cadence management.
- [Mailshake](https://mailshake.com/) - Simple cold email and follow-up automation.
- [Woodpecker](https://woodpecker.co/) - Cold email tool geared toward agencies and B2B teams.

## LinkedIn & Social Selling

- [Waalaxy](https://www.waalaxy.com/) - LinkedIn + email automation sequences.
- [Expandi](https://expandi.io/) - Cloud-based LinkedIn automation with safety-focused sending limits.
- [Dux-Soup](https://dux-soup.com/) - Browser-based LinkedIn prospecting and automation tool.
- [Sales Navigator](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator) - LinkedIn's native prospecting and lead-list tool.

## Landing Pages, Forms & Lead Capture

- [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/) - Drag-and-drop landing page builder with A/B testing and AI copy tools.
- [Instapage](https://instapage.com/) - Landing page platform focused on ad-to-page personalization.
- [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) - Conversational forms and surveys for lead capture.
- [OptinMonster](https://optinmonster.com/) - Popup and on-site conversion tools for capturing visitors as leads.
- [Leadpages](https://www.leadpages.com/) - Landing pages and lead-capture widgets for small businesses.

## Chatbots & Conversational Marketing

- [Drift](https://www.drift.com/) - Conversational marketing and chat-based lead routing.
- [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) - Customer messaging platform with lead-qualifying bots.
- [Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/) - Live chat and chatbot builder for small-to-mid-size sites.
- [Landbot](https://landbot.io/) - No-code conversational chatbot builder for lead qualification.

## CRM & Pipeline Management

- [HubSpot CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) - Free-tier CRM tightly integrated with marketing and sales tools.
- [Salesforce Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/sales-cloud/) - The dominant enterprise CRM platform.
- [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/) - Visual, pipeline-first CRM built for small sales teams.
- [Close](https://close.com/) - CRM with built-in calling and email, aimed at SMB sales teams.
- [Attio](https://attio.com/) - Flexible, data-model-driven CRM popular with startups.

## Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

- [Demandbase](https://www.demandbase.com/) - ABM platform for account identification, targeting, and orchestration.
- [6sense](https://6sense.com/) - Intent data and predictive analytics for account-based go-to-market.
- [RollWorks](https://www.rollworks.com/) - ABM platform aimed at mid-market teams.
- [Terminus](https://terminus.com/) - Account-based marketing and advertising orchestration.

## Cold Calling & Dialers

- [Orum](https://www.orum.com/) - AI-assisted parallel dialer for outbound sales teams.
- [Kixie](https://www.kixie.com/) - Power dialer and SMS integrated with popular CRMs.
- [Aircall](https://aircall.io/) - Cloud phone system with sales-dialer features.

## Analytics & Attribution

- [HockeyStack](https://hockeystack.com/) - B2B marketing analytics and revenue attribution.
- [Dreamdata](https://dreamdata.io/) - B2B revenue attribution across the full customer journey.
- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/) - Free web analytics, still a baseline for most funnels.
- [Hotjar](https://www.hotjar.com/) - Heatmaps and session recordings for conversion diagnosis.

## SEO & Content-Led Growth

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) - SEO research suite for keyword and content-gap analysis.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) - SEO, content, and competitive research platform.
- [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/) - On-page content optimization against ranking factors.
- [Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io/) - Content optimization for search intent and topical coverage.

## Paid Acquisition

- [Google Ads](https://ads.google.com/) - Search and display advertising for high-intent lead capture.
- [LinkedIn Ads](https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/ads) - B2B-targeted advertising by job title, company, and industry.
- [Meta Ads Manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads) - Facebook/Instagram advertising, strong for B2C and top-of-funnel B2B.
- [Capterra / G2 Ads](https://www.g2.com/) - Intent-based advertising on software review/comparison platforms.

## Frameworks & Playbooks

- [Predictable Revenue methodology](https://predictablerevenue.com/) - The outbound sales-development framework originating from Aaron Ross's work at Salesforce.
- [Bowtie Funnel](https://winningbydesign.com/) - Revenue-model concept from Winning by Design that extends the funnel past the sale into expansion.
- [Gap Selling](https://gapselling.com/) - Consultative sales framework focused on problem diagnosis over pitching.
- [Challenger Sale](https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/insights/challenger-sale) - Framework built on teaching, tailoring, and taking control of the sales conversation.
